☕ 365 Coffee Café – Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze 365 Coffee Café's sales and business performance.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping understand revenue trends, customer traffic, product performance, and store-level contributions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly revenue trends and sales performance.
- Identify peak customer traffic hours.
- Evaluate product category contributions.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare sales performance across different store locations.
- Generate meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

📊 Key Business Insights

1. Revenue Growth: Revenue increased by approximately 15% from January to June, showing a strong upward trend.

2. Peak Customer Traffic: Customer activity is highest during morning hours, particularly between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

3. Top Product Categories: Coffee (39%), Tea (30%), and Bakery (15%) collectively account for 84% of total transactions.

4. Sales Performance: June recorded the highest revenue, while February had the lowest revenue during the analyzed period.

5. Product Analysis: Identified the top 15 products based on revenue contribution.

6. Store Performance: Compared product category contributions across different store locations and identified areas with lower performance.
